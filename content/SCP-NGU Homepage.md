# Where am I?
You have found yourself in the midst of the SCP Next Generation Universe. In this alternate timeline, the world is pretty much the same, except for one small but glaring detail. Throughout the existence of mankind, there have been numerous reports on things that are otherwise unexplainable using traditional methods. Most of these things get rationalized and explained through more advanced scientific methods. However, for those that still remain unexplained, they get deemed as "Anomalies". Because of their unpredictable behavior, these anomalies must be Secured, Contained and Protected by a world-spanning NGO known as the S.C.P. Foundation, colloquially named, "The Foundation".
## What is The Foundation?
[[The Foundation]] is the aforementioned organization tasked with keeping anomalies at bay, maintaining the status quo, and most of the time, preventing the end of reality as we know it. The Foundation's reach truly is worldwide. With countless facilities, or sites located across many nations, The Foundation is capable of containing anomalies and accommodating each ones specific needs. In addition, The Foundation is intertwined into all aspects of society. Through shadow agents, government implants, and advanced internet crawlers, the foundation is able to get hints of suspected anomalies and dispel any conspiracies before the information gets widespread. The Foundation is made up of many different departments, organized by a group known as the "O5 Council", and is headed by a mysterious individual only known as, "The Administrator".
