# Description
SCP-NG-006 is a linguistic-based informational hazard operating in the realm of what The Foundation calls meta-lingophysics (which is by no means a recognized term in the English language). The complete form of SCP-NG-006 is unknown and any attempts to ascertain the complete form of SCP-NG-006 have resulted in increasingly complex metaphysical construct "jails" in which the observer becomes trapped in. In layman's terms, SCP-NG-006 is the idea that a sentence exists that is crafted in such a way as to elicit a certain effect from the observer. This can range from harmless feelings of fear and concern, to lethal cognitohazards that induce schizophrenia, delusions, psychosis, and even esoteric metaphysical constructs in which the observer is placed within. The closest kins to SCP-NG-006 are the popular \[REDACTED] text, and the litmus sentence for the english alphabet, "\[REDACTED].". While these two things do not elicit the effects of SCP-NG-006, they serve as examples as to the style and way in which an SCP-NG-006 concept would be synthesized from.

# Specialized Containment Procedures
Any written examples of text that evoke SCP-NG-006 are to be stored in a standard containment warehouse capable of keeping safe non-sentient anomalies contained. These written texts are to be laminated in vanta-black covering and stored face down in a secured location. Logs are to be kept in accordance to standard operating procedure for the containment site in which they are stored in and any usage of a text is to be logged as well. Should SCP-NG-006 become activated, MTF-(amnestics and obfuscation team) should be dispatched to reduce further spread. Should said occurrence occur through digital means, the text should be confiscated and added to the repertoire of SCP-NG-006 related agents, and then immediately scrubbed from where it was originally discovered.

## Appendix A-1 Testing Policy
### SCP-NG-006 Testing Policy
Due to the nature of SCP-NG-006, testing can prove difficult. In a typical test, an object that triggers SCP-NG-006 must not be opened and revealed by Foundation staff. Instead, SCP-NG-006's opening and viewing must be delegated to a Class-D subject.
