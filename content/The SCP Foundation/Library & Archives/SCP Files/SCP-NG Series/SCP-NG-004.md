# Description
SCP-NG-004 is an uncontained spatial cognitohazard with a disruption class of () and a risk class of (). NG-004 is located in and exists alongside a remote town in \[REDACTED – Insufficient Clearance]. The anomaly centers around this town (approximately 0.5 miles from any bordering house) (designated SCP-NG-004-A), and a room within a room in a house within this town (designated SCP-NG-004-B). NG-004's anomalous effects are pseudo-amnestic in nature. Subjects that live within the town tell stories and "facts" about the current reality that are not true. It is understood that subjects who enter the town begin to have their memories suppressed and replaced with false ones over the course of thirty hours, and is currently irreversible. The only location within the town where the anomalous effects cease to exist is within the house located at \[REDACTED – Insufficient Clearance]. Subjects who enter the upstairs bedroom in the house before the first 30 hours experience a gradual reclamation of their original memories, and the feeling felt while in this room is described as "...the feeling of waking up on a Saturday morning in the summer of 2009.". Those who enter the room after the thirty hours experience no reclamation of memories and continue to recount false memories and have delusions. There is no known method to restore a subjects original memories once they surpass thirty hours within the town. More research must be done to understand the full extent of the anomalous properties of SCP-NG-004-(A,B).

# Specialized Containment Procedures
As SCP-NG-004 is inherently impossible to "contain", a similar approach is taken as in the case of [[SCP-RE-3008|SCP-RE-3008]]. SCP-NG-004 is to be surrounded by a perimeter of municipal and public safety workers 1 mile out from the radius of the anomalies effects to detour any visitors. There is to be a 1:3 ratio of armed personnel at any given time, and a cover story must be given which in short explains that there is a chemical spill which causes undesirable hallucinogenic effects upon exposure. 
Subjects who try to leave the town must be interrogated to verify their exposure to the anomaly. If a subject is deemed curable, they are directed to the restoration room, given contextual amnestics therapy, and then civilian reintegration protocols are followed, ensuring the subject is smoothly reintegrated into society without arousing suspicion. If a subject is deemed incapable of reintegration, they are directed back into the town. If a subject resists, they are terminated. Subjects should not be terminated while living in the town, nor should one converse with any subjects under the influence of SCP-NG-004. The info they provide is false, and it should be regarded as "nonsense".  
# Appendices
## Appendix A-1 Testing Policy
### SCP-NG-004 Testing Policy
SCP-NG-004 is currently in a stable and concealed state. Testing can occur only on non-human subjects at this time. Any test involving human subjects is strictly prohibited without prior written and signed approval from either O5-3 or O5-10, and the respective department head. Testing and research is limited to the realm of the memory replacement and restoration effects. Any other kind of test or research is not permitted.
